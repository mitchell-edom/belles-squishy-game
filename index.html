🎮 Keyboard Controls

  • A or D
    Run: Move Belle left or right!
  • Spacebar or ▲
    Jump: Bounce off the ground or let go of swinging bars!
  • Q or 1
    Backflip: Perform a full 360° back flip!
  • W or 2
    Frontflip: Perform a 360° front flip!
  • E or 3
    Split: Strike a horizontal leg split!
  • R or 4
    Tuck: Curl knees to chest in a tight spin!
  • T or 5
    Layout: Straighten out in a clean gymnastics form!
  • O
    Reach/Grab: Reach hands to catch steel swing bars and rings!
  • R
    Restart: Start over at the beginning.
🏆 Tricks combo multiplier: Perform multiple tricks in a single jump before landing! Land upright (feet down) to secure your points. Landing upside down will cause a wipeout!
